SARI-SARI SMART (ANDROID) — END OF DAY TUTORIAL (6 STEPS)
=========================================================
Config: TutorialOverlay.kt -> pageTutorials ("eod", prefix "eodTutorial")
Page: closing (all steps)
Launch: Closing header (?) button -> End of Day Tutorial (startPageTutorial("eod"))

NOTE: Android page tutorials generate steps with NO highlight targets, so
the box stays at bottom center and no highlight frame is drawn.

STEP 1 — Closing page intro
  Key      : eodTutorial1
  Text EN : "This is the End-of-Day Closing page where you finalize your daily store operations."
  Text FIL: "Ito ang End-of-Day Closing page kung saan mo tatapusin ang iyong pang-araw-araw na operasyon sa tindahan."

STEP 2 — Summary
  Key      : eodTutorial2
  Text EN : "The summary shows today's total revenue, expenses, gross profit, and net profit at a glance."
  Text FIL: "Ang summary ay nagpapakita ng kabuuang revenue, expenses, gross profit, at net profit para sa araw na ito."

STEP 3 — Utang Breakdown
  Key      : eodTutorial3
  Text EN : "The Utang Breakdown section shows sales made on credit, pending profit from credit sales, and your actual cash profit for the day."
  Text FIL: "Ang Utang Breakdown section ay nagpapakita ng mga credit sales, pending profit, at ang iyong aktwal na cash profit."
  Interaction : read text, tap Next
STEP 4 — Checklist
  Key      : eodTutorial4
  Text EN : "Use the checklist to confirm you've counted your cash, checked your stock, and recorded any customer payments."

  Key      : eodTutorial5
  Text EN : "Tap the checklist items to mark them as complete. The system tracks your progress."
  Text FIL: "I-tap ang checklist items para markahan silang kumpleto. Sinusubaybayan ng system ang iyong progreso."
  Interaction : read text, tap Next
STEP 6 — Complete Day
  Key      : eodTutorial6
  Text EN : "When you're ready, tap \"Complete Day\" to finalize. This saves your end-of-day data and prepares for the next day."
  Text FIL: "I-tap ang \"Complete Day\" para tapusin. Ine-save nito ang iyong end-of-day data para sa susunod na araw."
  Interaction : read text, tap Finish (page tutorial finishes in place)

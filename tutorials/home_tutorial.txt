Launch: Morning header (?) button -> in-place launch (startPageTutorial("home"));
        Settings -> Tutorial selector / Help -> Tutorial selector (navigates here if needed)

SHARED STEP FIELDS
- Highlight appearance: pure outline — transparent center (element stays visible), white 2dp inner ring +
  green 4dp outer ring (offset 2dp outward), rounded corners ~12-14dp, -4dp offset around the target
- Navigation: Previous appears from step 2 onward; Next advances; on the last step the button reads
  "Get Started" and finishes in place; Skip (left side) + backdrop tap dismiss only on replay
- Box position rule: no highlight -> bottom center; target center in top 45% of screen -> card at bottom;
  otherwise card at top (below the header pill)
- Auto-scroll: when the step's highlight target is off-screen, the page scrolls to reveal it (where the
  screen provides a scroll state)

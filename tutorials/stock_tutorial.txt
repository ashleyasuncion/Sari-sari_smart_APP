SARI-SARI SMART (ANDROID) — STOCK TUTORIAL (10 STEPS)
=====================================================
Config: TutorialOverlay.kt -> pageTutorials ("stock", prefix "stockTutorial")
Page: inventory (all steps)
Launch: Inventory header (?) button -> Stock Tutorial (startPageTutorial("stock"))

NOTE: Android page tutorials generate steps with NO highlight targets, so
the box stays at bottom center and no highlight frame is drawn.

STEP 1 — Stocks page intro
  Key      : stockTutorial1
  Text EN : "This is the Stocks page where you manage all your inventory items."
  Text FIL: "Ito ang Stocks page kung saan mo pinamamahalaan ang lahat ng iyong inventory item."
  Interaction : read text, tap Next
STEP 2 — Search bar
  Key      : stockTutorial2
  Text EN : "Use the Search bar to quickly find any item by typing its name."
  Text FIL: "Gamitin ang Search bar para mabilis na makahanap ng item sa pamamagitan ng pag-type ng pangalan nito."
  Interaction : look at the search bar, tap Next
STEP 3 — Add Stock
  Key      : stockTutorial3
  Text EN : "Tap \"Add Stock\" to add new products or restock existing ones. Fill in the name, quantity, cost per unit, and selling price. You can set a markup percentage to auto-calculate the selling price."
  Text FIL: "I-tap ang \"Add Stock\" para magdagdag ng bagong produkto o mag-restock ng existing item na may detalye tulad ng pangalan, dami, halaga, at presyo."
  Interaction : see the Add Stock button, tap Next
STEP 4 — Running Low section
  Key      : stockTutorial4
  Text EN : "Items running low appear in the \"Running Low\" section at the top, showing remaining quantity and a quick \"Add Stock\" button."
  Text FIL: "Ang mga item na nauubos ay lilitaw sa \"Kulang Na\" section sa itaas, na may natitirang dami at mabilis na \"Add Stock\" button."
  Interaction : look at the Running Low section, tap Next
STEP 5 — Color-coded inventory
  Key      : stockTutorial5
  Text EN : "All your inventory is listed below with color-coded borders: green = plenty, orange = getting low, red = out of stock."
  Text FIL: "Ang lahat ng iyong inventory ay nakalista sa ibaba na may color-coded na border: green = marami pa, orange = medyo kulang, red = wala na."
  Interaction : look at the item list, tap Next
STEP 6 — Item card
  Key      : stockTutorial6
  Text EN : "Each item card shows the name, status indicator, quantity on hand, profit margin percentage, and a \"Sold\" button to quickly deduct stock when you make a sale."
  Text FIL: "Ang bawat item card ay nagpapakita ng pangalan, status indicator, dami, at \"Sold\" button para mabilis na bawasan ang stock."
  Interaction : look at an item card, tap Next
STEP 7 — Product page
  Key      : stockTutorial7
  Text EN : "Tap any item card to open its dedicated Product page showing cost price, selling price, profit margin, and stock status. This is where you manage individual item details."
  Text FIL: "I-tap ang kahit anong item card para buksan ang Product page nito. Makikita mo ang halaga, presyo ng benta, profit margin, at stock status. Dito mo pinamamahalaan ang detalye ng bawat item."
  Interaction : read text, tap Next
STEP 8 — Product page actions
  Key      : stockTutorial8
  Text EN : "On the Product page, you can deduct stock when items are sold, update the stock status (Plenty, Getting Low, or Out of Stock), or tap \"Edit\" to modify item details. Stock updates are reflected immediately."
  Text FIL: "Sa Product page, maaari mong bawasan ang stock kapag may naibenta, i-update ang stock status (Marami Pa, Medyo Kulang, Wala Na), o i-tap ang \"Edit\" para baguhin ang item details."
  Interaction : read text, tap Next
STEP 9 — Add Stock page
  Key      : stockTutorial9
  Text EN : "The Add Stock page lets you add new products or restock existing ones. Enter the item name, quantity, cost per unit, and selling price. The markup helper suggests the right selling price automatically."
  Text FIL: "Ang Add Stock page ay kung saan mo pinupunan ang pangalan ng produkto, dami, halaga, at presyo ng benta. Ang markup helper ay awtomatikong nagbibigay ng tamang selling price."
  Interaction : read text, tap Next
STEP 10 — Save product
  Key      : stockTutorial10
  Text EN : "Fill in all the fields and tap \"Save\" to add the product to inventory. The markup percentage helps you maintain consistent pricing across similar products."
  Text FIL: "Punan ang lahat ng field at i-tap ang \"Save\" para idagdag ang produkto sa inventory. Ang markup percentage ay tumutulong sa iyo na mapanatili ang consistent na pricing."
  Interaction : read text, tap Finish (page tutorial finishes in place)

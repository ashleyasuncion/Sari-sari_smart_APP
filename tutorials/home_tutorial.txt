SARI-SARI SMART (ANDROID) — HOME TUTORIAL (10 STEPS)
====================================================
Config: TutorialOverlay.kt -> pageTutorials ("home", prefix "homeTutorial")
Page: morning (all steps)
Launch: Morning header (?) button -> Home Tutorial (startPageTutorial("home"))

NOTE: Android page tutorials generate steps with NO highlight targets, so
the box stays at bottom center and no highlight frame is drawn (differs
from the web app, which highlights each element).

STEP 1 — Home dashboard
  Key      : homeTutorial1
  Text EN : "This is your Home dashboard — your store's command center. Get a complete snapshot of your business at a glance."
  Text FIL: "Ito ang iyong Home dashboard — ang command center ng iyong tindahan. Makita ang kumpletong snapshot ng iyong negosyo."
  Interaction : read text, tap Next
STEP 2 — Today's Earnings
  Key      : homeTutorial2
  Text EN : "Today's Earnings shows your total cash sales and profit (credit/utang sales excluded). Tap it to go to the Sales page."
  Text FIL: "Ang Kita Ngayon ay nagpapakita ng iyong kabuuang benta at kita. I-tap ito para pumunta sa Sales page."
  Interaction : look at the earnings card, tap Next
STEP 3 — Stock Alert
  Key      : homeTutorial3
  Text EN : "Stock Alert tells you how many items are running low. Tap it to go to the Stocks page."
  Text FIL: "Ang Stock Alert ay nagsasabi kung ilang item ang nauubos na. I-tap ito para pumunta sa Stocks page."
  Interaction : look at the stock alert, tap Next
STEP 4 — Outstanding Debts
  Key      : homeTutorial4
  Text EN : "Outstanding Debts shows the total amount customers owe you. Tap it to go to the Utang page."

  Key      : homeTutorial5
  Text EN : "End-of-Day Closing helps you complete your daily store routine: count cash, check stock, record customer payments, and finalize the day's summary with revenue, profit, expenses, and cash profit breakdown."

STEP 6 — Closing page intro
  Key      : homeTutorial6
  Text EN : "This is the End-of-Day Closing page where you finalize your daily store operations. Complete three steps: count your cash drawer, check your stock levels, and record any customer payments received today."

  Key      : homeTutorial7
  Text EN : "Step 1: Enter how much cash is in your drawer right now. This helps you verify that your actual cash-on-hand matches what the system recorded from today's sales."

  Key      : homeTutorial8

  Key      : homeTutorial9

STEP 10 — Complete Closing
  Key      : homeTutorial10
  Text EN : "When all steps are complete, tap \"Complete Closing\" to finalize your day. This saves your end-of-day summary and prepares your store for tomorrow."

  Interaction : read text, tap Finish (page tutorial finishes in place)

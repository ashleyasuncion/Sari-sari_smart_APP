SARI-SARI SMART (ANDROID) — NEW SALE TUTORIAL (5 STEPS)
=======================================================
Config: TutorialOverlay.kt -> pageTutorials ("newSale", prefix "newSaleTutorial")
Page: day (all steps — the New Sale sheet opens from Day Mode)
Launch: New Sale page -> Tutorial selector / Help selector (startPageTutorial("newSale"))

NOTE: Android page tutorials generate steps with NO highlight targets, so
the box stays at bottom center and no highlight frame is drawn.

  Key      : newSaleTutorial1
  Text EN : "This page lets you record a specific sale for notable items or credit/utang transactions."

STEP 2 — Product name & quantity
  Key      : newSaleTutorial2
  Text EN : "Enter the item or product name and the quantity sold."
  Text FIL: "Ilagay ang pangalan ng item at ang quantity na naibenta."
  Interaction : look at the fields, tap Next
STEP 3 — Automatic total
  Key      : newSaleTutorial3
  Text EN : "The total amount is calculated automatically based on the product's selling price."
  Text FIL: "Ang total amount ay awtomatikong kinakwenta batay sa selling price ng produkto."

STEP 4 — Customer name (utang)
  Key      : newSaleTutorial4
  Text EN : "If the customer is buying on credit (utang), enter their name here. This will automatically create a debt record."
  Text FIL: "Kung ang kostumer ay bumibili on credit (utang), ilagay ang kanilang pangalan dito. Awtomatikong lilikha ito ng debt record."

STEP 5 — Save Sale
  Key      : newSaleTutorial5
  Text EN : "Tap \"Save Sale\" to record the transaction. If a customer name was entered, it will also appear in the Utang page."
  Text FIL: "I-tap \"Save Sale\" para itala ang transaction. Kung may customer name, lalabas din ito sa Utang page."
  Interaction : read text, tap Finish (page tutorial finishes in place)
